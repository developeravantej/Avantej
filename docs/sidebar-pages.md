# Sidebar Pages Reference

## Overview

### Home
Dashboard landing page. Shows a high-level snapshot of the business — KPI cards, recent activity, and widgets like claims and announcements.

### Reports
Aggregated analytics and reporting across sales, attendance, commissions, and other business metrics.

---

## Customers

### Registered
List of customers who have signed up and have a profile in the system. Manage customer details, membership status, and history.

### Walk-in
Unregistered / walk-in customers who were served without an account. Track their visits and purchases separately from registered members.

### Memberships
Active and past membership records tied to registered customers. View plan type, start/end dates, and status.

### Package Instances
Individual service package instances purchased by customers — each row represents one customer owning one package (e.g. "10-session PT pack").

### Installments
Customers who are paying for packages in instalments. Track payment schedules, amounts due, and completion status.

---

## Scheduling

### Appointments
One-on-one or small-group appointments booked by or for customers. View, create, and manage bookings with trainers or staff.

### Class Slots
Group class sessions with a capacity limit. Staff can manage class schedules and see how many slots are filled or available.

### Rooms
Physical rooms or spaces within the branch. Used when scheduling appointments or classes that require a specific room.

---

## Services

### Service Categories
Top-level groupings for services (e.g. "Personal Training", "Spa", "Yoga"). Organisational layer that services belong to.

### Services
Individual services offered to customers (e.g. "60-min Deep Tissue Massage"). Linked to a category and used when booking or selling packages.

---

## Sales

### Sales
All sales orders — new, completed, and pending. The primary record of every transaction made at the branch.

### Payments
Payment transactions tied to sales orders. Track payment method, amount collected, and outstanding balances.

### Cancelled Sales
Sales orders that were voided or cancelled. Kept for audit and refund reference.

### Promotions
Discount codes and promotional campaigns. Create, enable, and track promo codes that customers or staff can apply at checkout.

### Purchase Orders
Orders placed with suppliers to restock inventory. Track order status from draft through to fulfilment.

---

## Inventory

### Products
All physical products stocked and sold by the branch (retail items, supplements, etc.).

### Inventory Options
Configuration for inventory behaviour — e.g. low-stock thresholds, reorder settings.

### Brands
Product brand registry. Used to tag and filter products by manufacturer or brand.

### Categories
Product categories (separate from service categories). Used to organise inventory items.

### Suppliers
Supplier/vendor directory. Linked to purchase orders to track where stock comes from.

### Unit of Measure
Base units used to measure products (kg, litre, piece, etc.).

### UoM Conversions
Conversion rules between units of measure (e.g. 1 box = 12 pieces). Needed for accurate stock tracking.

### Stock Requests
Internal requests from staff to move or replenish stock — e.g. a branch requesting items from a warehouse.

### Transfer Orders
Approved transfers of stock between locations or warehouses. Follows from a stock request.

---

## Finance

### Expenses
Branch operational expenses — rent, utilities, supplies, etc. Log and review spending outside of payroll.

### Payouts
Payouts owed to staff or trainers — e.g. commission disbursements or freelancer payments.

### Refunds
Refund records for cancelled or disputed sales. Track what was refunded, to whom, and when.

### Commissions
Commission records earned by staff or trainers based on sales or sessions. View breakdowns per person or period.

---

## Staff

### Trainers
Staff members with a trainer role. Manage their profiles, assigned services, and schedules.

### Employees
All staff members in the branch (non-trainer roles). View and manage HR-level records.

### User Roles
Role definitions used within the branch (e.g. Manager, Receptionist, Trainer). Separate from the access control permission system.

### Attendance
Staff clock-in / clock-out records. Monitor punctuality and working hours.

### Audit Log
System-level log of all significant actions taken in the platform — who did what and when. Used for compliance and investigation.

---

## Access & Comms

### Role Management
Create and edit permission roles that control what staff can see and do in the system.

### Permissions
Assign specific permissions to each role — read/write access per feature area.

### User Assignment
Assign staff members to a role and a hierarchy node (branch, region, etc.) to define their access scope.

### Invitations
Send email invitations to new staff members to join the organisation in the platform.

### Announcements
Broadcast messages to staff across the organisation or branch. Displayed in the dashboard and notifications.

### Notifications
In-app notification centre for the current user — alerts, reminders, and system messages.

---

## Organisation

### Branches
All physical branches (locations) in the organisation's hierarchy. View and manage branch details, parent region, and settings.

---

## Settings

### Tax Rates
Configure GST, SST, or other tax rates applied at checkout. Set a default rate for the branch.

### Installment Plans
Define the instalment plan templates available to customers — number of payments, intervals, and fees.

### Google Calendar
Connect the organisation's Google Calendar account for appointment sync.

### Organization
Core organisation profile settings — name, logo, contact info, and subscription details.

### Notifications
Configure notification preferences — which events trigger alerts and how they are delivered.

### GHL Integration
GoHighLevel CRM integration settings. Connect the branch's GHL account for lead and contact sync.

---

## My Work _(staff/trainer only)_

### My Services
Personal view for trainers and staff — shows the services they are assigned to deliver and their upcoming sessions.

---

## Platform Admin _(platform admins only)_

### Users
Super-admin view of all users across all tenants on the platform.

### Admins _(super admins only)_
Manage other platform-level admins and their roles.
