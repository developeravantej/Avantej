1. Getting Started

Signing in, setting up your gym, and finding your way around.

Signing in

  1. Open your gym's Avantej web address in any browser.
  2. Enter your email and password, then select Sign in.
  3. If you were invited by email, use the link in that invitation to set your password the first time.

You can also be sent a one-time sign-in link by email if you forget your password.

Setting up a new gym (onboarding)

When your organisation is first created, an onboarding wizard walks you through entering your gym details, branches, and first staff members. You can return to setup at any time until it is complete.

Finding your way around

  • The left sidebar is your main menu — every area in this guide lives there.
  • The dashboard Overview shows your key numbers (sales, appointments, members) for today, this week, this month, or this year.
  • What you see depends on your role. A cashier sees fewer options than a manager.
Tip: If you were just given new permissions and don't see a menu item, sign out and back in to refresh your access.

2. Customers

Your directory of everyone who buys from or trains at the gym.

Sidebar → Customers

You can:

  • Add a new customer — record their name, phone, and email.
  • Add a walk-in customer — for one-off buyers who aren't members.
  • Edit a customer's name or email at any time.
  • Open a customer's profile to see their purchases, packages, and appointments.
  • Invite a customer to claim their account — send them a sign-up link or magic link by email so they can log in to the member app.
  • Group customers into a household — link family members together and add or remove people.
  • Remove a customer (soft delete) and restore them later if needed.
  • Record where a customer came from using lead sources.

How to add a customer

  1. Go to Customers in the sidebar.
  2. Select Add customer.
  3. Enter their name, phone, and email, then save.
  4. Optionally select Send claim link so they can access the member app.

3. Members & Memberships

Staff accounts and ongoing membership plans.

Sidebar → Access Control · Memberships

Staff members

  • Add a staff member and assign their role.
  • Suspend, reactivate, or deactivate a staff member.
  • Change a member's role or branch scope — what they're allowed to see and do.
  • Remove and restore a member account.

Memberships

  • View membership plans and who is enrolled.
  • Freeze a membership when a customer pauses their plan.

4. Services

The treatments, sessions, and classes you sell.

Sidebar → Services · Service Categories
  • Create a new service — e.g. "Body Massage 60min" with its price and duration.
  • Edit or duplicate a service to quickly build similar ones.
  • Set a different price per branch — and expire or remove a branch price later.
  • Organise services into categories and create new categories.
  • Assign which trainers can deliver a service and set buffer times between sessions.

How to create a service

  1. Go to Services and select Create service.
  2. Enter the name, duration, and standard price.
  3. Choose a category.
  4. Save, then assign trainers from the trainer's profile if needed.

5. Appointments

Booking and managing one-on-one sessions.

Sidebar → Appointments
  • Book an appointment for a customer with a chosen service, trainer, and time.
  • Reschedule or update an existing appointment.
  • Cancel a single appointment, all future ones, or a whole recurring series.
  • Split a recurring series when only part of it needs to change.
  • Reinstate session credits when an appointment is cancelled.
  • Bulk-create appointments from a schedule for recurring bookings.
Tip: Leave the trainer blank to let the rotation queue assign one automatically.

6. Classes & Coaching

Group class slots and coach intake scheduling.

Sidebar → Appointments → Class Slots
  • Create a class slot with a time, capacity, and trainer.
  • Update or cancel a class slot.
  • Enrol a client into a class, or cancel their enrollment.
  • Set up coach intake slots and let coaches claim or release them.

7. Attendance & Check-ins

Recording who showed up.

Sidebar → Attendance
  • Check a customer in when they arrive — one at a time or in bulk.
  • View today's bookings and check-in history.
  • See a member's active package at the moment of check-in.
  • Remove a check-in recorded by mistake.

8. Point of Sale (POS)

Ringing up sales at the front desk.

Sidebar → POS (Sales → Create)
  • Record a sale — add services, products, or packages and take payment.
  • Search for a customer during checkout, or sell to a walk-in.
  • Apply a promotion or tax at checkout.
  • View the sales list with separate tabs for installments, payments, cancelled sales, and refunds.
  • Open a sale's receipt to see the full detail.
  • Cancel a sale when needed.

How to ring up a sale

  1. Go to Sales → Create.
  2. Search for and select the customer (or choose walk-in).
  3. Add the services, products, or package to the cart.
  4. Apply any promotion, then confirm the payment method.
  5. Record the sale — the receipt is generated automatically.
Important: When selling a package on an installment plan, the POS shows the installment amount due now, not the full package price.

9. Packages

Pre-paid bundles of sessions or services.

Sidebar → Packages · Package Instances
  • Sell a package to a customer (creates a package instance).
  • Freeze, unfreeze, or extend a package.
  • Cancel or reject a package.
  • Assign a trainer to a package and manage package staff.
  • Confirm payment and review pending packages.
  • Bulk-assign packages to multiple customers at once.
  • Sign the package agreement and upload or download payment proof.

10. Installment Plans

Letting customers pay over time.

Sidebar → Packages → Installments · Settings → Installment Plans

Managing a customer's installments

  • View the installment schedule for a package and upcoming dues.
  • Confirm a payment or reschedule an installment.
  • Waive an installment or adjust credits.
  • Suggest and process a refund on installments.
  • Set an overdraft, manually unlock, or reset overrides for special cases.
  • Export upcoming installments to a CSV file.

Setting up plan templates

  • Create installment plan templates (e.g. 3-month, 6-month).
  • Set a default plan, and deactivate or reactivate plans.

11. Payments & Refunds

Collecting money and giving it back.

Sidebar → Sales & Payments · Refunds

Payments

  • Send a payment link for a sale or an installment.
  • Set up a card on file for a customer.
  • View payment history and reconcile records.

Refunds

  • Request a refund (full or partial).
  • Approve or reject a refund request — single or in bulk.
  • Process an approved refund back to the customer.

12. Products & Inventory

Retail items, brands, and units.

Sidebar → Products · Brands · Categories · UOM
  • Add and edit products for retail sale.
  • Set branch-specific prices for products.
  • Manage product categories and brands — create, edit, delete.
  • Define units of measure and conversion rules (e.g. box → bottle).

13. Commissions

Tracking what trainers and staff earn on sales.

Sidebar → Commissions · My Commissions
  • View commission splits earned across the gym, or just your own.
  • Approve, void, or mark a commission paid — one at a time or in batches.
  • Contest a commission and resolve disputes or reviews.
  • Make a manual commission adjustment for special cases.
  • Set commission cycles and category rates.
  • Export the commission liability to a CSV file.
Note: Commission always reaches the trainer — whether they were specifically requested or assigned by the rotation queue.

14. Payouts

Paying out what staff have earned.

Sidebar → Approvals (Operations)
  • Run a payout batch for a period.
  • Process a payout for one recipient.
  • Mark a payout paid or failed, retry, or cancel it.

15. Trainers

Managing your coaching team.

Sidebar → Trainers
  • View trainers, their weekly schedule, and weekly stats.
  • Set trainer availability and add exceptions (days off).
  • Check trainer utilisation — how booked they are.
  • Manage trainer tiers — create tiers and assign them to staff.

16. Promotions

Discounts and promo codes.

Sidebar → Promotions
  • Create a promotion with a value and validity dates.
  • Edit or deactivate a promotion.
  • Validate a promo code at checkout.

17. Expenses

Recording money the gym spends.

Sidebar → Expenses
  • Record an expense and categorise it.
  • Approve or reject a submitted expense.
  • Mark an expense as paid.

18. Announcements & Notifications

Keeping staff and members informed.

Sidebar → Announcements · Notifications
  • Post an announcement — create, edit, or delete.
  • Read your notifications and mark them read (one or all).
  • Set your notification preferences — what you get alerted about.

19. Reports

Understanding how the business is doing.

Sidebar → Reports · Overview
  • View business KPIs on the dashboard overview by period.
  • Open detailed reports for sales, commissions, and more.
  • Schedule reports to be emailed automatically — create, toggle, or delete schedules.

20. Staff Access & Roles

Controlling who can do what.

Sidebar → Access Control
  • Create and edit roles (e.g. Cashier, Manager) and delete unused ones.
  • Set each role's permissions — add or remove what they can access.
  • Assign roles to users.
  • Invite new staff by email — resend, revoke, or accept invitations.
Tip: System administrators bypass all permission checks. After changing someone's permissions, they may need to sign out and back in to see the change.

21. Settings & Integrations

Configuring how the gym runs.

Sidebar → Settings
  • Organisation profile — your gym's name, branding, and details.
  • Branches & rooms — create branches, manage rooms and locations.
  • Tax rates — create rates, set a default, deactivate.
  • Installment plans & commission cycles — payout schedules and plan templates.
  • Email settings — verify sending domains, manage aliases, and route notifications.
  • Google Calendar — connect, sync, or disconnect.
  • GoHighLevel (CRM) — connect your organisation or a branch and retry failed syncs.

22. Audit Log

A record of who did what.

Sidebar → Audit
  • View the audit log of actions taken in the system.
  • Filter to one customer's history.
  • Export the log to a CSV file.

23. Platform Administration

For Avantej operators who manage multiple gyms (tenants). Most gym staff will not see this area.

Sidebar → Platform Admin
  • Manage tenants — provision, suspend, resume, cancel, issue credit, or re-trial a gym; request exports and deletions.
  • Billing & coupons — view billing, create and apply coupons.
  • Communications — send direct messages and announcements (single or bulk), manage email templates.
  • Payments oversight — handle disputes, payment drift, and run reconciliation.
  • Revenue & health — revenue KPIs, churn, cohort retention, renewal calendar, health scoreboard.
  • System — review migrations, webhook events, and audit actions; impersonate a user for support.
