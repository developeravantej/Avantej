# Installment Function Enhancements

Three coordinated improvements to the installment system: a Due Soon view for proactive payment collection, a per-package-instance overdraft flag that lets admins allow specific customers to keep booking despite a missed payment, and making the already-implemented grace period visible in the admin detail sheet.
