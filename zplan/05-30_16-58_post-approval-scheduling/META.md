# Post-Approval Session Scheduling

Closes the gap between "package approved / trainer assigned" and "sessions actually on the calendar". Adds a Schedule Sessions step (driven by member preferred times + trainer availability), an availability cross-check at assignment time, a one-click Reschedule action, and a No-show → reinstate-credit-and-reschedule follow-through. For gym admins, trainers, and front-desk staff on the Avantej platform.
